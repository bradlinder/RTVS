Notice of Separate Executable Invocation:
----------------------------------------
Radio & TV Segmenter invokes FFmpeg and ffprobe as separate standalone external
executable subprocesses via standard command-line pipes to perform audio
extraction, format conversion, and waveform generation. Radio & TV Segmenter does
not link directly to FFmpeg C libraries.
